How to pour
Every tool button opens its own drop down. Tap a tool to pick what it pours, shakes or drops.

Pour lays down the liquid you pick: steamed milk, chocolate milk, caramel cream and more. Hold still to grow a blob, drag to push it across the cup. Its drop down also has Wiggle, which rocks the pour side to side for rosettas, and Dots, which lays a chain of spots.

Stick is the etching tool. It lays down no color and only drags the foam, so pulling it straight through a blob turns it into a heart. Pick a needle, a stick or a spoon.

Syrup drizzles thin lines of chocolate, caramel and more. Drizzle lines, then drag the stick across them for the classic feathered mocha look.

Shake dusts cinnamon, cocoa, salt and friends. Top drops whipped cream, cherries, candy canes and chocolate chunks. Drag right after you drop one to slide it into place.

Pick a target, pour, then tap Finish to score it against the guide and save it to your log.
